Why This, Why Now

Half of Saudi Arabia's disease burden comes from conditions people can change: what they eat, how they move, whether they connect. The kingdom spends billions treating what it could prevent. Vision 2030's Quality of Life program has built the rails: Seha, Tawakkalna, NPHIES, national sports infrastructure. What's missing is the layer that turns those rails into neighbourhood-level health behaviour change.

The Gap

Saudi Arabia has world-class hospitals. It does not yet have a system that measures, motivates, and rewards healthy behaviour at the neighbourhood level. That gap costs ~3.5% of GDP in preventable burden.

The Pilot

Season 1: Foundation

6 neighbourhoods, 2 clusters. Stand up the yardstick, test engagement, baseline the score.

Between Seasons

Tune the AI engine. Adjust weights. Partner feedback loop.

Season 2: Optimization

Double down on what moved the score. Scale reward partnerships. Add employer wellness tie-ins.

End-of-Pilot Readout

DALYs averted, cost per QALY, NPS, engagement rates. The business case for national scale.
