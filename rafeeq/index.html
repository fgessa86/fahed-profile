The shift

HHC only meets people when they are sick.

Saudi Arabia has world-class hospitals. But 80% of what determines health happens outside them: in neighborhoods, in daily routines, in the choices people make at home.

🏥

Today

A business of hospitals and clinics. Most of the population HHC is responsible for never walks in. 95% of spending goes to treatment.

🏘️

Under capitation

HHC carries the cost of illness for a population it holds cradle to grave. Keeping people well stops being marketing. It becomes how HHC protects its business.

Every healthy day is margin.

Insurers already act on this. Vitality pays for wellness rewards out of lower claims. HHC has the same incentive and a far larger population that does not switch.

Key assumptions

  • 8,000 residents per neighborhood; 30% active participation rate
  • Saudi DALY cost of SAR 48,200 (burden-of-disease data)
  • Published relative risk reductions for lifestyle interventions (diabetes 12%, CVD 8%)
  • Late-stage treatment costs SAR 60K more per case than early detection
  • 720 screenings per season at 3.3% detection rate = 24 early catches
  • Average daily wage SAR 430; presenteeism at 50% of absenteeism
  • Program cost SAR 150K per neighborhood per season; scales down with shared infrastructure
  • Conservative estimates. The model sharpens as real data replaces assumptions.

The pilot

Six neighborhoods. Two seasons.

Start small, prove the model, then expand. Enough time for two full competition cycles and a credible dataset.

Season 1: Foundation

Launch in 6 neighborhoods across 2 HHC clusters. Onboard residents, activate sponsors, run the first league competition. Baseline all six health dimensions.

Between seasons

Analyze what moved the score. Tune the AI nudge engine. Expand sponsor coalition based on Season 1 engagement data.

Season 2: Optimization

Second competition cycle with refined scoring and expanded partner integration. Measure delta from baseline. Build the expansion case.

End-of-pilot readout

Full impact assessment: DALYs averted, cost savings, engagement rates, NPS, and the case for scaling Rafeeq across all HHC clusters.
