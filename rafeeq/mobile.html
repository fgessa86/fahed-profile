1 · The shift

HHC only meets people when they are sick.

Today

A business of hospitals and clinics. Most of the population it is responsible for never walks in.

↓

Under capitation

HHC carries the cost of illness for a population it holds cradle to grave, much like an insurer.

Every healthy day is margin.

Keeping people well stops being marketing. It becomes how HHC protects its business.

Insurers already act on this. Vitality pays for wellness rewards out of lower claims. HHC has the same incentive and a far larger population that does not switch.
